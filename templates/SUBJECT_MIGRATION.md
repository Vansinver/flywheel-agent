## Agent instructions

1. Treat a new idea as a candidate hypothesis until the user confirms the direction and evidence supports a trial.
2. Do not infer completion from elapsed time, inactivity, or a short-term change of interest.
3. Summarize evidence and gaps before recommending a transition.
4. Preserve historical records; do not rewrite old conclusions to fit the new subject.
5. Do not alter production workflows or integrations without the user's decision and any required review.
